RepIO is a music player made for practising. Learn a piece bit by bit: mark the difficult passages, loop them, slow them down – and speed them up again once you've got them.

Sections and markers
• Define as many sections as you like (start and end point), play them once or in a loop
• Set markers and jump to them directly
• Name sections and markers, edit start, end and tempo at any time
• All sections and markers in one list, sorted by time and shown on the timeline

Tempo
• Playback speed from 50 % to 200 % without changing the pitch
• Store a tempo per section – e.g. practise the solo at 70 %, the rest at full speed
• Back to 100 % with a single tap

Analysis
• For every section: tuning (concert pitch, e.g. 442 Hz), tempo in BPM and key
• Tempo and key suggestions with a colour-coded confidence indicator; the chosen tempo is shown in the list, converted to your practice speed
• Runs entirely on the device

Made for practising
• Large controls you can hit with an instrument in your hands
• Playback continues with the screen locked
• History of the last 20 pieces; the last piece is preselected on start
• Adjustable accent colour, English and German interface

Your files, your data
• Plays local audio files (MP3, FLAC, OGG, WAV and more)
• Sections and markers are saved as a readable YAML file next to the audio file – easy to back up, copy or edit
• No internet access, no ads, no tracking, no account
• Free and open source
